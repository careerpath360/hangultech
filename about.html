About us

A technology company that builds and runs software.

Founded in 2023, Hangul is a technology company. Some of what we build, we keep and operate ourselves — the rest is software we build and run for other teams, across whatever industry they're in.

Our approach

We build software we can operate, not just ship.

Hangul is, first, a technology company — a team of engineers who build and run software. Some of that work becomes our own products: EduHan, MediHan, HangoTrip, HayaKart and Hanzio are the ones in our portfolio today, spanning education, healthcare, travel, retail and home services. The rest of our work goes into building and running software for other teams, under the exact same standard: it has to work for the person using it every day, not just look good in a demo.

That means real attention to the boring parts — attendance that syncs correctly, queues that don't lose their place, orders that reconcile, payments that land. We treat operational reliability as a feature, because for the people relying on this software, it is the feature.

Whether it's a product we own or one we're building for a client, it's held to the same bar.

What guides how we build

  • Built for real operations
    Designed around the day-to-day workflow of the people who actually use it — parents, patients, vendors, providers.
  • One team, shared standards
    Every product shares the same engineering, security and testing practices, regardless of which industry it serves.
  • Simple over clever
    We'd rather ship something dependable than something impressive that breaks under real usage.
  • In it for the long run
    We build products we intend to operate and support, not hand off and forget.

Our values

What we hold ourselves to

01

Reliability first

Software that people depend on has to work every single day — not most days.

02

Security & privacy

Student records, patient data and payment details are handled with the care they require, by default.

03

Straight talk

We'd rather tell you what's realistic than what's easy to hear — with clients and with each other.

04

Own the outcome

We measure our work by whether it actually holds up in production, not whether it shipped on time.

05

Design for the user, not the demo

The person filling in a form or checking a queue matters more than the person watching a pitch.

06

Keep learning

Five different industries have taught us five different sets of lessons — we bring all of them to every new build.
